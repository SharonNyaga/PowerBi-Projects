# NexaMart Retail Analytics — Business Brief

## Company
NexaMart is a fictional Kenyan retail business operating stores across six regions.

## Management request
Management wants a Power BI report that explains sales and profitability performance, identifies underperforming regions/products/stores, compares actual performance with targets, and helps managers investigate customer and product performance.

## Core questions
1. How much revenue and profit are we generating?
2. Are we meeting sales and profit targets?
3. Which regions and stores perform best/worst?
4. Which products/categories drive revenue and profit?
5. Are discounts affecting profitability?
6. Which customer segments are most valuable?
7. How is performance changing over time?
8. Where should management investigate further?

## Important
The raw files intentionally contain a small number of data-quality issues. 

## Expected Power BI workflow
Data ingestion → Power Query → data model → DAX measures → visuals → advanced interactions → RLS → performance review → business recommendations.
